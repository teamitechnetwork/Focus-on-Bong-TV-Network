# PROJECT: FOCUS ON BONG TV NETWORK — OFFICIAL WEBSITE

I want you to design, develop, and launch a world-class, professional media and television broadcasting website for **FOCUS ON BONG TV NETWORK**.

Official brand name: Focus on Bong TV Network

Tagline: The Heartbeat of Bong County, Connecting the World.

Brand identity: Inform. Educate. Entertain. Connect.

I will upload the official company logo. Use the uploaded logo as the primary brand identity throughout the website. Do not redesign, distort, recolor, or replace the logo. Maintain its original proportions and quality.

## 1. PROJECT VISION

Build a modern, premium, visually impressive digital media platform that represents an established television and news broadcasting organization.

The network will cover news and stories from Bong County, across Liberia, Africa, and around the world.

The website must accommodate:
- Breaking news and latest developments.
- National and international news.
- Politics and governance.
- Business and economy.
- Education and opportunities.
- Sports.
- Entertainment and lifestyle.
- Culture and tourism.
- Community development.
- Investigative journalism.
- Interviews and documentaries.
- Video reports and live broadcasting.

The website must communicate credibility, professionalism, authority, and trust.

Create a premium television-network-inspired interface.

Create a distinctive visual identity that makes Focus on Bong TV Network recognizable.
